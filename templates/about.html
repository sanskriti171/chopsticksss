Our Story

Chopsticks Restaurant Leominster

A Culinary Tradition

Welcome to Chopsticks Restaurant Online, Chinese Takeaway in Leominster. Located at 17 Etnam Street, we have been serving the Leominster community with high-quality, authentic Chinese food for years.

Our experienced chefs use fresh ingredients, traditional spices, and time-honored cooking techniques to prepare classic Chinese dishes. From our comforting chicken sweet corn soup and crispy aromatic duck to our savory curries and stir-fries, we make sure each dish is cooked to perfection.

We partner with Meals 4U to provide a seamless online ordering system, making it easy for you to browse our menu, place takeaway orders, or schedule a home delivery service.
